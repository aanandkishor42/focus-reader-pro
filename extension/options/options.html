Focus Reader Pro

Reading settings

Local only

Control what Focus Reader may store and where it should never run. Your settings and reading history stay on this device.

Reading log

Logging is off by default. When enabled, the extension records the page title, URL, domain, word count, estimated minutes, and time.

One domain per line. A blocked domain also blocks its subdomains. Lines may contain comments with #.

Focus appearance

Text size and line width are adjusted from the extension popup while you read. Open the popup, then use the Focus Mode controls.

Your data

History is stored locally by the Focus Reader API. Clear it at any time. Nothing is uploaded to a remote service.

Focus Reader Pro keeps reading data local unless you export it.
